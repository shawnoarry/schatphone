# Familiar and brand sound sources

These files are bundled for local, non-commercial SchatPhone prototyping. They are kept in a separate
brand-sound directory so ringtone and notification semantics can share the same source without mixing
the settings layers.

- `nokia-tone.mp3`: community-uploaded Nokia Tune from Myinstants: https://www.myinstants.com/en/instant/nokia-tune-90928/
- `iphone-marimba.mp3`: community-uploaded iPhone Marimba from Myinstants: https://www.myinstants.com/en/instant/iphone-marimba-90618/. The bundled copy removes the source file's first 0.967 seconds of silent MP3 frames, reducing it from 8.464 seconds to about 7.5 seconds.
- `old-phone-ring.mp3`: community-uploaded Western Electric-style old phone ring from Myinstants: https://www.myinstants.com/en/instant/old-phone-ringtone-73400/. The selected source is already a short 6.6-second, loop-suitable excerpt.
- `sony-ericsson-morning-glow.mp3`: community-uploaded Sony Ericsson Morning Glow from Myinstants: https://www.myinstants.com/en/instant/morning-glow-73734/
- `samsung-over-the-horizon.mp3`: Samsung Newsroom media asset from the Over the Horizon brand-sound article: https://img.global.news.samsung.com/global/wp-content/uploads/2020/10/Over_the_Horizondefault_ringtone.mp3
- `samsung-atomic-bell.mp3`: Samsung Newsroom media asset from the Galaxy sound collection: https://img.global.news.samsung.com/global/wp-content/uploads/2020/10/Atomic_Bell2nd_sim_default_ringtone.mp3
- `samsung-80s-phone.mp3`: Samsung Newsroom media asset from the Galaxy sound collection: https://img.global.news.samsung.com/global/wp-content/uploads/2020/10/80s_Phone.mp3
- `samsung-arcade.mp3`: Samsung Newsroom media asset from the Galaxy sound collection: https://img.global.news.samsung.com/global/wp-content/uploads/2020/10/Arcade.mp3. It is offered as a ringtone only.
- `samsung-whistle.mp3`: community-uploaded Samsung Whistle sound mirrored through Voicemod Tuna: https://us-tuna-sounds-files.voicemod.net/edf152c9-b570-431c-9fdf-9a5a0b31c103-1666145485265.mp3. It is used as a notification option only.

The familiar and Samsung-derived files are offered as local prototype assets and are not presented as SchatPhone originals.
